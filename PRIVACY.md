# Privacy policy for ASCII Vision

Last updated: November 7, 2026

ASCII Vision does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## What the extension does with your data

- **Page content and images:** the extension reads the page you are viewing, including its images and videos, only to turn them into text on your own device. This content is never sent to the developer or to any server.
- **Image downloads:** to read the pixels of images hosted on other websites, the extension's background script downloads that image directly from its original address, the same address your browser was already loading, and passes it to the page on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Settings:** your choices (on/off, color mode, detail) are saved with the browser's built-in `chrome.storage.sync`. If you are signed in to your browser, the browser may sync these settings between your own devices. The developer has no access to them.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect browsing history.
- It does not record what you type, including passwords and form fields.
- It does not use cookies.
- It does not send any data to third parties.

## Permissions

The extension asks to run on all websites because it restyles whichever page you open. All processing happens locally in your browser.
